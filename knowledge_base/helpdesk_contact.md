# IT Helpdesk Contact Information

For all IT-related issues, employees should contact the IT Helpdesk.

**Helpdesk Phone Number:** 1-800-555-0100

The IT Helpdesk is available Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM EST. For after-hours emergencies, please use the same number and follow the prompts for emergency support.

You can also reach the helpdesk via email at helpdesk@acmecorp.com or through the internal ticketing portal at https://support.acmecorp.internal.
