# Password and Security Policy

## Password Requirements

- Minimum 12 characters
- Must include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters
- Passwords expire every 90 days
- Cannot reuse your last 10 passwords

## Multi-Factor Authentication

All employees must enroll in MFA using the Okta Verify app. MFA is required for:
- VPN access
- Email access from personal devices
- Admin portals

## Reporting Security Incidents

If you suspect a security incident (phishing email, unauthorized access, lost device), immediately report it to the Security Team at security@acmecorp.com or contact the IT Helpdesk.

## Phishing

Do not click links in suspicious emails. Forward suspected phishing emails to phishing@acmecorp.com. The security team conducts regular phishing simulations for training purposes.
