# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Overview

AcmeCorp reimburses employees for reasonable business expenses incurred during the course of their work.

## Submitting Expenses

1. Log in to Concur at https://concur.acmecorp.internal
2. Create a new expense report
3. Attach receipts for all expenses over $25
4. Submit for manager approval within 30 days of the expense

## Approval Limits

- Up to $500: Direct manager approval
- $500 - $2,000: Director approval required
- Over $2,000: VP approval required

## Travel

Book all travel through the Concur travel portal. Preferred airlines and hotels are listed in the travel policy section. Per diem rates follow GSA guidelines.

## Questions

For expense policy questions, contact the Finance team at finance@acmecorp.com.
