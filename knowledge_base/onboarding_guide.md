# New Employee Onboarding Guide

Welcome to AcmeCorp! This guide will help you get started during your first week.

## Day 1 Checklist

1. Pick up your laptop and badge from the front desk (Building A, Room 101)
2. Complete your HR paperwork in Workday
3. Set up your email and Slack accounts using the credentials in your welcome packet
4. Attend the 10:00 AM orientation session in Conference Room B

## IT Setup

Your laptop comes pre-configured with standard software. If you need additional software or have technical issues, contact the IT Helpdesk or submit a ticket through the support portal.
